# BeefCake Bell manual

## About the Bell

The BeefCake Bell is an adjustable kettlebell from 4 kg to 32 kg in 2 kg steps. It sits in a base cradle, and you change the weight with the dial on top of the handle. The Bell connects to the BeefCake Coach app over Bluetooth to count reps and track weight.

## Changing the weight

Set the Bell fully in its base cradle on a flat floor. The dial only turns when the Bell is seated in the cradle. Turn the dial until it clicks at the weight you want, then lift the Bell straight up. The plates you're not using stay in the cradle.

## The weight dial is stuck or won't click

If the dial won't turn or won't click at a weight, lift the Bell out of the cradle and set it back in so it sits flat. Check that the cradle is on a flat, hard floor and that nothing is caught between the plates. A solid amber light on the handle means the Bell isn't seated properly. Don't force the dial, and don't spray lubricant into the dial or the cradle. If the dial still won't click after reseating the Bell, contact BeefCake Support.

## Lights on the handle

Flashing blue: ready to pair. Solid blue: connected to the Coach app. Flashing red: battery low. Solid amber: the Bell isn't seated in the cradle properly.

## Pairing the Bell with the Coach app

Press and hold the button under the handle for 3 seconds until the light flashes blue. In the Coach app, go to Devices > Add device > Bell. Keep your phone within 2 meters while pairing.

## Battery and charging

The Bell has a rechargeable battery that charges over USB-C through the port under the handle. A full charge takes about 2 hours and lasts about 30 workouts. The battery drains faster if the Bell stays connected to the app when you're not using it. Put the Bell back in its cradle after a workout and it will go to sleep after 5 minutes.

## Firmware update failed

Charge the Bell to at least 50 percent, keep your phone within 1 meter, and try the update again from Devices > Bell > Update in the Coach app. If it fails twice, force close the Coach app, open it again, and retry.

## Care

Wipe the handle with a damp cloth. Keep the Bell dry and don't use it outdoors. Don't drop the Bell from overhead.
