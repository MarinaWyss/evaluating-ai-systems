# BeefCake Row manual

## Setting up the Row

Unfold the rail until it clicks into place, then tighten the locking knob under the seat track. Plug the power adapter into the socket at the front base of the Row and into a wall outlet. The power switch is on the front base, next to the power socket. Switch it on and the screen will start within about 30 seconds. Follow the on-screen steps to choose your language and create a profile.

## The Row won't turn on

Check that the power adapter is firmly connected at both ends and that the outlet works by plugging in something else. Look at the small light on the power adapter. A green light means the adapter is getting power. No light means the adapter or outlet is the problem. Make sure the power switch on the front base is set to on. If the adapter light is green, the switch is on, and the screen still stays dark, contact BeefCake Support.

## The screen is frozen or not responding

Press and hold the power button on the side of the screen for 5 seconds to restart the screen. Your workout history is saved and won't be lost. If the screen freezes again during the same session, switch the Row off at the front base, wait 10 seconds, and switch it back on.

## Resetting the Row

To restart the Row, use the steps for a frozen screen. To reset the Row to factory settings, go to Settings > System > Factory reset on the screen. A factory reset removes all local profiles and paired devices, but workouts already synced to the Coach app are kept.

## Resistance feels wrong

Resistance is set with the dial on the right side of the rail, from level 1 to level 20. If every level feels the same, or the resistance doesn't match the level on the screen, calibrate it. Go to Settings > Resistance > Calibrate, then row 10 easy strokes when the screen asks you to. Calibration takes about a minute. If the resistance still feels wrong after calibrating, contact BeefCake Support.

## Connecting the Row to the Coach app

The Row connects to the BeefCake Coach app over Bluetooth. On the Row, go to Settings > Connections > Coach app and select Pair. In the Coach app, go to Devices > Add device > Row. If the Row loses its connection to the Coach app during a workout, keep your phone within 3 meters of the Row, turn Bluetooth off and on again on your phone, and restart the Coach app. If the connection still drops, remove the Row in the Coach app under Devices and pair it again.

## Showing your heart rate on the Row

The Row screen can show your heart rate from a BeefCake Pulse strap. Put on the strap, then go to Settings > Connections > Heart rate on the Row and select your Pulse.

## Care and maintenance

Wipe the rail and seat with a dry or slightly damp cloth after each workout. Don't use cleaning sprays on the screen. Once a month, check the pull strap for fraying. Don't oil the rail.

## Specifications

Maximum user weight: 150 kg. Screen: 16 inch touchscreen. Resistance: 20 levels. Folded footprint: 60 by 90 cm.
