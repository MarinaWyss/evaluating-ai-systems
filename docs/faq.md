# BeefCake FAQ

## Do I need a Coach subscription to use the Row or the Bell?

No. The Row works in Just Row mode without a subscription, and the Bell works as a normal adjustable kettlebell. Coach adds guided classes, rep counting, and workout history.

## Can I use the Pulse with other apps?

The Pulse works with the BeefCake Row and the BeefCake Coach app.

## Is BeefCake equipment safe if I have an injury or a health condition?

We can't give medical advice. If you have an injury, pain, or a health condition, talk to your doctor before starting a new exercise routine.

## How much space do I need for the Row?

Allow about 2.5 by 1 meters of floor space while rowing. The Row folds to 60 by 90 cm for storage.

## Where is my serial number?

On the Row, it's on a sticker under the front base. On the Bell, it's under the handle. On the Pulse, it's inside the battery cover.
