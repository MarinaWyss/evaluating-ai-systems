# BeefCake Pulse manual

## Wearing the Pulse

The BeefCake Pulse is a chest strap heart-rate monitor. Wet the two electrode pads on the back of the strap with water, then wear the strap just below your chest muscles with the BeefCake logo facing out. Tighten it so it stays in place without being uncomfortable.

## Pairing the Pulse with the Row

Put on the strap so it wakes up. On the Row screen, go to Settings > Connections > Heart rate and select your Pulse from the list.

## Pairing the Pulse with your phone

Pair the Pulse through the Coach app, not through your phone's Bluetooth settings. Put on the strap, open the Coach app, and go to Devices > Add device > Pulse. If the Pulse doesn't appear, make sure it isn't already connected to your Row, because the Pulse connects to one device at a time.

## Signal drops or keeps disconnecting

If your heart rate drops out or the Pulse keeps disconnecting, wet the electrode pads again and tighten the strap. Keep the device it's paired with within 2 meters. The Pulse connects to one device at a time, so disconnect it from the Row before using it with your phone. A weak battery also causes the signal to drop, so replace the battery if the problem continues.

## Heart-rate readings seem wrong

Readings can spike or drop in the first few minutes, before you start to sweat. Wet the electrode pads, move the strap so it sits just below the chest muscles, and make sure the logo faces out. A loose strap is the most common cause of wrong readings.

## Replacing the battery

The Pulse uses one CR2032 coin battery, which lasts about 6 months with regular use. Open the battery cover on the back of the sensor with a coin, replace the battery with the positive side facing up, and close the cover firmly.

## Care

Rinse the strap in clean water after each workout and let it air dry. Unclip the sensor before washing the strap. Don't put the sensor in the washing machine.
