# BeefCake Coach app help

## About Coach

BeefCake Coach is a subscription app for iOS and Android with guided classes for the Row and the Bell. It records your workouts and syncs data from the Row, the Bell, and the Pulse.

## Logging in

Log in with the email address you used to sign up. If you forgot your password, tap Forgot password on the login screen and follow the link in the email. The link expires after 1 hour.

## Free trial and subscription

New members get a 14-day free trial. Your subscription starts automatically when the trial ends, and you're billed monthly on the same date each month. To avoid being charged, cancel before the trial ends.

## Seeing two charges

If you see two charges from BeefCake Coach, one of them may be a temporary authorization hold from your bank, which usually disappears within 3 to 5 business days. If both charges are still there after 5 business days, contact BeefCake Support with the dates and amounts of both charges so we can look into it.

## Cancelling your subscription

Go to Profile > Subscription > Cancel subscription. You keep access until the end of the billing period you've already paid for. We don't refund partial months.

## Syncing workouts

Workouts from the Row, the Bell, and the Pulse sync to Coach over Bluetooth. Open the Coach app after your workout and keep your phone nearby until the sync finishes. If a workout is missing, pull down on the History screen to refresh. Check that the device shows as connected under Devices.

## Getting help

Contact BeefCake Support from the Coach app under Profile > Help. Our support team can look up orders, start returns, and check warranty status.
