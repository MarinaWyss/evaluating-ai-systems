# BeefCake policies

## Returns

You can return any BeefCake hardware (the Row, the Bell, or the Pulse) within 30 days of delivery, whether it's been opened and used or not. Include all parts and accessories. The customer pays return shipping, unless the item arrived damaged, in which case we send a prepaid return label (see Damaged deliveries below). We refund the original payment method within 10 business days of receiving the return. To start a return, contact BeefCake Support.

## Warranty

The BeefCake Row and the BeefCake Bell have a 2-year warranty. The BeefCake Pulse has a 1-year warranty. The warranty starts on the delivery date and covers defects in materials and workmanship. It doesn't cover normal wear, damage from misuse, or water damage. The warranty covers the original purchaser only and can't be transferred. To make a warranty claim, contact BeefCake Support with your serial number.

## Coach subscription

BeefCake Coach includes a 14-day free trial. You can cancel any time. When you cancel, you keep access until the end of the current billing period. We don't give refunds for partial months.

## Damaged deliveries

If your order arrives damaged, contact BeefCake Support within 7 days of delivery with photos of the damage. We'll send a replacement or a prepaid return label. Damage reported after 7 days is handled as a warranty claim.
